# Parent Guide

A practical walkthrough of the parent dashboard (`/parent`).

## Signing in
Sign in at `/login`. You'll land on `/parent`, showing a summary for your child (or children, if you have more than one enrolled).

## Viewing your child's profile
`/parent/child` — your child's enrollment details, class, program, guardian and emergency-contact information on file (contact the school admin to correct anything here).

## Attendance
`/parent/attendance` — a calendar/list view of your child's daily attendance (present/absent/late), as marked by their teacher.

## Homework
`/parent/homework` — assignments for your child's class, due dates, and status. Mark an assignment submitted once your child has completed it.

## Daily activities
`/parent/activities` — a running feed of what your child did each day (learning, play, meals, nap, art, outdoor), including any photos/videos their teacher attached.

## Messaging your child's teacher (or the school admin)
`/parent/messages` — start or continue a 1:1 conversation with your child's teacher or a school admin. You'll see typing indicators and online status, and messages arrive live if the other person is online. You can attach photos/documents, react to messages, and reply to a specific message in the thread.

## Fees
`/parent/fees` — view invoices issued for your child, payment history, and outstanding balances. Download a PDF receipt for any payment.

## Events — RSVP
`/parent/calendar` (and the public `/events` page) — see upcoming school events and RSVP with your name, email, and number of guests attending.

## Tracking an admission application
If you're applying for a new child (or tracking an application you submitted before creating an account), `/parent/admissions` shows the current status of your application: Submitted → Under Review → Accepted / Waitlisted / Rejected.

## Announcements
`/parent/announcements` — notices posted by your child's teacher or the school admin.

## Downloads
`/parent/downloads` — school forms, circulars, or documents made available for download.

## Gallery
`/parent/gallery` — browse the school's public photo/video albums.
