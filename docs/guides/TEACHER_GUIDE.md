# Teacher Guide

A practical walkthrough of the teacher dashboard (`/teacher`).

## Signing in
Sign in at `/login` with your teacher account. You'll land on `/teacher`, showing your assigned class at a glance.

## Marking attendance
`/teacher/attendance` — pick a date, mark each student in your class Present, Absent, or Late, and optionally add a note. Parents can see their child's attendance history immediately after you save.

## Assigning homework
`/teacher/assignments` — create a homework assignment for your class (subject, title, description, due date). Parents/students see it appear on their side, and it's automatically flagged overdue once the due date passes without a submission.

## Logging daily activities
`/teacher/activities` — log what a child did during the day (learning, play, meals, nap, art, outdoor time), with an optional note and photo or video attached. Parents see these logged activities as a running feed for their child.

## Posting announcements
`/teacher/announcements` — post a notice to your class (or the whole school/parent body, depending on the audience you choose). Everyone in the target audience sees it on their dashboard.

## Messaging parents
`/teacher/messages` — 1:1 conversations with the parents of the children in your class (or with admins). Messages arrive instantly if the other person is online; otherwise they see it the next time they open the app. You can attach files, react with emoji, reply to a specific message, and pin important messages within a conversation.

## Other pages
- `/teacher/students` — the roster of children in your class, with quick access to each child's profile.
- `/teacher/progress` — track each student's progress notes.
- `/teacher/calendar` / `/teacher/events` — upcoming school events.
